## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Modernize `SafeMath`

**User Story:** As the owner of `SafeMath`, I want it upgraded without changing its behavior, so that existing users keep working and legacy risks are removed.

#### Acceptance Criteria

2. **1.2 (Ubiquitous)** The modernized `SafeMath` contract SHALL compile with Solidity 0.8.0 or later so that arithmetic overflow reverts.

### Requirement 3: Modernize `LegacyVault`

**User Story:** As the owner of `LegacyVault`, I want it upgraded without changing its behavior, so that existing users keep working and legacy risks are removed.

#### Acceptance Criteria
